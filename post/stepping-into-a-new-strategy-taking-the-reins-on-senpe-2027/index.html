Stepping into a New Strategy: Taking the Reins on SENPE 2027

Cristobal Bozdogan · Oct 21, 2026

Every major congress transition brings an exciting professional turning point. Taking over commercial operations and sponsorship management for SENPE 2027 (the 42nd National Congress of the Sociedad Española de Nutrición Clínica y Metabolismo) marks an intentional step into a dynamic, highly specialized space within medical event management.

Having inherited this key national account from a long-time colleague, my goal is clear: build upon a strong foundation, refine our commercial offerings, and deliver maximum strategic value for both the client board and our industry sponsors.

Expanding Multilingual & Cross-Cultural Strategic Leadership

Adapting to distinct client cultures and linguistic environments is central to how I drive international business. Having previously led committee coordination and stakeholder operations in French for global meetings like ICOPA in Canada, stepping into SENPE marks an exciting milestone: taking full commercial ownership of my first completely Spanish national congress. Managing high-level B2B sales, board relations, and corporate pitch strategies entirely in Spanish allows me to showcase my agility as a fully bilingual strategist, tailoring touchpoints directly to the domestic healthcare market.

Scaling Personal Leadership Across Legacy Accounts

Taking the lead on an established national congress requires a deliberate balance of continuity and innovation. The account came with strong, long-standing industry trust. My focus has been stepping seamlessly into board-level conversations, establishing proactive touchpoints with key pharmaceutical and nutrition leaders, and positioning myself as a reliable, strategic partner. It is a prime opportunity to demonstrate how high-level relationship management drives commercial continuity.

Modernizing Commercial & Operational Frameworks

Inheriting an account from a previous management setup inherently opens doors for structural optimization. By integrating standardized, high-efficiency workflows, commercial tracking systems, and modernized sponsorship models, I am streamlining how we manage booth allocations, symposia packages, and corporate reporting. This structural alignment elevates the entire client and sponsor experience, bringing data-driven execution to the forefront.

Setting the Stage for Zaragoza 2027

Looking toward 2027, we are bringing healthcare leaders together at the Palacio de Congresos de Zaragoza—an architectural landmark in the former Expo 2008 precinct. Designing an expansive trade floor and structuring high-impact networking spaces in a venue of this scale allows me to combine strategic exhibition planning with immersive delegate experiences.

Driving Results Through Evolution

Navigating complex client dynamics, refining legacy systems, and leading high-value commercial portfolios are core to my approach. As sponsorship sales for SENPE 2027 ramp up, I look forward to delivering a highly profitable, seamlessly executed congress in Zaragoza.
